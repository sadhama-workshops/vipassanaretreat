A Silent Retreat Near London: Vipassana on the Thames in Oxfordshire

By Dmitry Glazkov, vipassana teacher · 3 min read

Many people write to me with the same question: is there a proper silent retreat near London, without flying to Thailand or waiting months for a place?

Yes. Our Insight Vipassana retreats happen at Hardwick Estate, on the banks of the River Thames in South Oxfordshire. It sits in the Chilterns National Landscape, formerly the Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty. Rolling hills, ancient oaks, wildflower meadows, and a clean stretch of the Thames.

How far is it?

  • By train: about 40 minutes from London Paddington to Reading or Pangbourne, then a short taxi.
  • By car: about 20 minutes from Junction 12 of the M4.
  • By air: Heathrow is about an hour by car.

That's close enough to come for a week without losing a day to travel, and far enough that you hear only birds and the river.

Why nature matters for meditation

Remember entrainment: we fall into the rhythm of wherever we are. Spend time in the City and you take its tempo. Spend a week at dawn in a misty meadow and the mind slows to the pace of the trees.

The Buddha himself practised and taught in forests, under trees, in parks and groves. Many of the verses in the Theragāthā, the poems of the early monks, are set in the wild: rain on a hut, peacocks calling, mountain streams. There's a reason.

At Hardwick, we meditate in an open marquee in the meadow, beside an old oak. Walking meditation happens on the grass. Between sessions, you can sit by the river.

What the retreat looks like

  • 7 days, with 5 full days of noble silence
  • Sitting and walking meditation in the Mahasi noting tradition
  • Daily yoga to prepare the body for sitting
  • Dhamma talks and personal interviews with the teacher
  • Plant-based Ayurvedic meals, much of it organic and grown on the estate by Tolhurst Organic
  • Riverside sauna on the first and last day, and wild swimming in the Thames
  • Camping in your own tent, campervan, or a pre-erected tent or bell tent you can rent at booking

It's simple living, but well looked after. Not a concrete bed in a monastery. Not a spa either. Something doable, so your energy goes into the practice.

Who is it for?

Beginners and experienced meditators. People who've done a ten-day Goenka course and want to try a different method. People who can't take ten days off work. People who want to practise in nature rather than indoors.

Dates for 2027

  • 31 May – 6 June 2027
  • 26 July – 1 August 2027
  • 30 August – 5 September 2027
