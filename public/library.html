The Dhamma library

Eight collections of the Buddha's discourses. Read online, download, or listen aloud.

These are the original teachings from the Pāḷi Canon that Dmitry draws on in his Dhamma talks, in Bhikkhu Sujato's clear modern translation, which he dedicated to the public domain. Free for everyone, forever.
